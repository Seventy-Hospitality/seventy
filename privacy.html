Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains how Seventy Hospitality ("we", "us") handles personal information when you use the Club 70 mobile app (the "App"), seventyhospitality.com (the "Site") and the memberships and bookings you make through them (together, the "Services").

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, password, and optionally your phone number, date of birth, display name and profile photo. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address they share with us (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one).
  • Membership and bookings: your membership tier and status, court bookings, players you invite or who invite you, groups you create or join, waivers you sign, and your check-ins at the club.
  • Payments: card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number; we receive the card brand, last four digits and expiry date, plus a record of your payments, refunds and credits.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you email or contact us.

Information collected automatically

  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, a push token for your device and its platform (iOS or Android), plus your notification preferences.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and app version information, and error reports when something goes wrong, so we can keep the Services secure and working.

The App does not use your location or contacts, does not include advertising, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It asks for access to your photos only when you choose a profile picture, and only uses the photo you pick. The Site has no forms, accounts, cookies or analytics.

How we use information

  • To create and run your account, membership and bookings, and to let you check in at the club.
  • To take payments, issue refunds and keep financial records.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders, membership and account notices, and other messages you choose to receive by push, email or text.
  • To show your name and photo to players you book with or share a group with.
  • To keep the Services secure, prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems and improve them.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and enforce our terms.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that run parts of the Services for us and may use the information only to do so: our club management platform, Clutch, which hosts accounts, memberships and bookings on Supabase and Vercel; Stripe (payments); Apple and Google (sign-in, and push notification delivery through Expo, Apple and Google); Resend (email); Twilio (text messages); Sentry (error monitoring); and, for the Site, GitHub Pages (hosting) and Google Fonts (typefaces).
  • Other members, limited to what the Services show them, such as your name and photo on a shared booking or in a group.
  • Authorities or others when the law requires it, or when needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our members, staff or the public.
  • A successor if our business or the club is sold or reorganized, subject to this policy.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information, except records we must keep for legal, tax or accounting reasons (such as payment records) or to resolve disputes, which we keep only as long as needed for those purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your profile in the App, or ask us for a copy of your information.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account in the App's account settings, or ask us to do it.
  • Notifications: you can turn push, email and text notifications on or off in the App's settings or your device settings. We may still send messages you cannot opt out of, such as payment receipts and account security notices.

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have additional rights under local law, such as to know what we collect, to correct or delete it, and not to be treated differently for exercising these rights. To make a request, email us; we will verify it is you before acting on it.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us if you suspect your account has been compromised.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and children cannot create their own accounts. A parent or guardian may add a child to their account to manage the child's bookings and programs; the parent or guardian controls that information and can ask us to delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is material we will tell you in the App or by email before it takes effect. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
